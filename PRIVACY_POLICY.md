# Merit-Trade AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Merit-Trade AI, its related services, and associated websites collect, use, share, and protect your personal information. By using the Merit-Trade AI platform, you agree to this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account and Registration Data
When you sign up for Merit-Trade AI, we collect information required to create and manage your account:
- Full name
- Email address
- Username
- Password (stored securely as a hash)
- Account preferences and profile information

### 1.2 Authentication and Session Data
We collect data related to access and authentication, including:
- Access tokens and refresh tokens
- Login timestamps
- IP address and browser headers for security and fraud detection

### 1.3 Trading and Signal Activity
To provide trading signals and execution features, we process:
- Requested symbols and asset classes
- Signal preferences and filters
- Trade orders, open/closed trade records
- Risk setting configuration and execution history

### 1.4 Brokerage and Exchange Credentials
If you connect a broker or exchange account, we store:
- Broker/exchange name
- Account number or label
- Connection settings such as server and demo mode indicator
- Credentials and API keys encrypted at rest using strong cryptography

### 1.5 Usage and Analytics Data
We automatically collect information about how you use the platform to improve performance and service quality:
- Page views and feature usage
- Session duration and navigation flow
- Error reports and device/browser metrics

### 1.6 Communication Preferences
If you enable alerts or notifications, we collect:
- Telegram chat IDs
- Email addresses and notification preferences
- Browser push subscription metadata

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:
- Create, authenticate, and manage your account
- Deliver AI trading signals and manage execution workflows
- Enforce risk rules and perform compliance checks
- Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
- Improve platform usability, performance, and security
- Send service notifications, alerts, and promotional messages (when permitted)

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Merit-Trade AI does not sell your personal information.
We may share data with third parties only when necessary to provide services or comply with the law:
- Service providers that support platform hosting, analytics, email delivery, or payment processing
- Brokers and exchanges when you link accounts for trade execution
- Legal authorities if required by law or to protect our rights and users
- Third parties during business transfers, acquisitions, or reorganizations, subject to confidentiality obligations

## 4. Security

We maintain technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data:
- Encryption of sensitive broker and exchange credentials using AES-256-GCM
- Password hashing using strong algorithms such as bcrypt
- Token handling with short-lived access tokens and refresh token flows
- Secure API access and authorization checks
- Regular monitoring for suspicious activity and security incidents

## 5. Cookies and Tracking

The platform may use cookies and similar technologies to support:
- Session management and login state
- Functional settings and preferences
- Performance monitoring and analytics

You can control cookie settings through your browser, but some functionality may be reduced if cookies are disabled.
